Plus Fitness, 2023

After a great deal of convincing my parents that going to the gym will not make me shorter, I moved on from those home workouts and begun my stint at Plus Fitness. I think I was trying to draw Thornleigh's Plus but I couldn't really remember how the dumbbell rack looks so I had to put in that little squat rack at the back to emphasise that this is in fact a gym. Many yap sessions were carried out here whilst narcisstically posing in-front of the mirrors.
