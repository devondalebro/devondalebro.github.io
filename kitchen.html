Hornsby Heights, 2017

This was the first house that my family bought in Australia. With a huge backyard that sloped over a hill, we got to raise chickens in the back and this was also when we bought my upright piano as we finally had enough space. It was also the first one with air-conditioning and I vividly remember watching the Lakers play before the Anthony Davis trade while getting blasted by cold air. I will never forget my time here as this was where I felt like I really belonged in Sydney.

This was actually the last scene I drew so it's probably the best as I hadn't drawn since high school before this.
