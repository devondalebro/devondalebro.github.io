Westleigh, 2025

This was inspired by the private basketball courts at my complex. The big wall at the back is actually holding a swimming pool so occasionally water would splash over while I was shooting my shots. I've been playing basketball since year 7 and will never stop #BallIsLife. Back in my religious days when I was just a little kid I used to pray everyday that I would grow to 7 foot so I can make the NBA but we all know how that turned out.

Still one of the earlier pieces I drew (the arrows are in order of drawing) so the shading is pretty uneven and crass which makes this look like a prison basketball court. Hey, I'm still warming up and stuff I haven't drawn in so long...
